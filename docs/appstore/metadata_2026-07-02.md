**Description (EN):**

```
Breathwave is a simple app for guided breathing and quiet meditation — and the wave on screen can be your actual breath. No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking.

YOUR BREATH, SENSED
Lie down, place the phone on your belly, and watch the wave rise and fall with your real breathing. Breathwave follows your breath with the motion sensor — no camera, no microphone, no wearable. Finish with your breathing rate and a steadiness score.

BREATH GARDEN
Every completed session grows a unique flower, generated from that session — its length, its kind, its rhythm. Over weeks, your practice becomes a garden no one else can grow.

IN THE DARK
Turn the screen almost black and let haptics and sound guide your breath. Close your eyes; the phone breathes with you.

BREATHING EXERCISES
• Coherent breathing (5.5 s) — balance and calm
• Box breathing 4-4-4-4 — focus
• 4-7-8 — wind down in the evening
• Extended exhale — quick relaxation
• Custom rhythm — set your own phases, save presets
• Om training — lengthen your exhale with a warm drone

A gentle wave guides your breath on screen, with soft sounds and haptics — it keeps working even with the screen off.

MEDITATION TIMER
Opening and closing gong, optional interval bells, calm ambient sound. Choose ocean surf, a gentle breeze, a quiet breathing sound — or silence.

YOUR PROGRESS
Your streak and minutes are stored only on your phone. Optionally, sessions are written to Apple Health as mindful minutes.

PRIVACY
Breathwave collects no data. Zero. It does not even connect to the internet.

Breathwave is free and has no ads. If it helps you, you can support development with a voluntary tip inside the app.
```

## App Review Notes (EN, do pole Notes v App Store Connect)

```
Breathwave 1.1 responds to the 4.3(a) feedback on our previous submission with three features we believe are not found together in this category:

1. BREATH SENSOR (Home -> "Breath Sensor" card): the app senses the user's actual breathing. Lie down and place the phone flat on the belly; the accelerometer picks up the rise and fall, and the on-screen wave mirrors the real breath. The session ends with a breathing rate and a steadiness score. To test at a desk: hold the phone flat in your palm and slowly raise and lower it (about 5 seconds up, 5 seconds down) to simulate belly movement. Uses raw CMMotionManager data only — no permission prompt, and no data leaves the device.

2. BREATH GARDEN (Home -> flower icon, top right): every completed session deterministically generates a unique procedural flower from that session's data (length, kind, breathing steadiness). The collection grows into a personal garden over time.

3. IN THE DARK (any breathing exercise -> "In the dark" toggle): the screen turns near-black and haptics plus sound guide the breath, so the practice works with eyes closed.

The app remains fully offline: no accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no network calls. The tip jar (Settings -> Support) is a voluntary consumable IAP that unlocks nothing.
```
