**Description (EN):**

```
Breathwave is a calm space for breathing, meditation — and staying present with the apps that pull you in. No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking.

MINDFUL PAUSE
Choose the apps that distract you. When you open one, Breathwave gently asks you to pause and take a breath first, then lets you through for as long as you decide. A small moment of focus before the scroll — so you open apps on purpose, not on autopilot.

BREATH GARDEN
Every completed session grows a unique flower, generated from that session. Over weeks your practice becomes a quiet garden that no one else can grow.

IN THE DARK
Dim the screen to near-black and let gentle haptics and sound guide your breath. Close your eyes; the phone breathes with you.

BREATHING EXERCISES
• Coherent breathing (5.5 s) — balance and calm
• Box breathing 4-4-4-4 — focus
• 4-7-8 — wind down in the evening
• Extended exhale — quick relaxation
• Custom rhythm — set your own phases, save presets
• Om training — lengthen your exhale with a warm drone

MEDITATION TIMER
Opening and closing gong, optional interval bells, calm ambient sound — ocean surf, a gentle breeze, a quiet breathing sound, or silence.

YOUR PROGRESS
Streak, minutes, and mindful pauses are stored only on your phone. Optionally, sessions are written to Apple Health as mindful minutes.

PRIVACY
Breathwave collects no data. Zero. It does not even connect to the internet. The apps you choose to pause never leave your device.

Breathwave is free and has no ads. If it helps you, you can support development with a voluntary tip inside the app.
```

## App Review Notes (EN)

```
Breathwave 1.3 adds "Mindful Pause", a focus feature built on FamilyControls / ManagedSettings / DeviceActivity for personal, individual device management on the user's own device.

How it works and how to test:
1. Home → "Mindful Pause". Tap "Get Started" and grant Screen Time access (individual authorization — not parental/MDM).
2. Choose one or more distracting apps via the system FamilyActivityPicker, set an unlock duration, and turn the pause on.
3. Open a chosen app: instead of the app, a calm "Take a breath" screen appears. Two buttons (their positions vary): one closes the app, the other opens it for the chosen number of minutes, after which the shield returns.

Purpose is focus and productivity (a mindful pause before distracting apps), not ad blocking or managing another person's device. App selection uses opaque tokens — the app never learns which apps were chosen, and nothing leaves the device (no network, accounts, or analytics). The tip jar (Settings) is a voluntary consumable IAP that unlocks nothing.
```
