**Description (EN):**

```
Breathwave is a simple app for guided breathing and quiet meditation. Open it, breathe, feel better. No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking.

BREATHING EXERCISES
• Coherent breathing (5.5 s) — balance and calm
• Box breathing 4-4-4-4 — focus
• 4-7-8 — wind down in the evening
• Extended exhale — quick relaxation
• Custom rhythm — set your own phases, save presets
• Om training — lengthen your exhale with a warm drone

A gentle wave guides your breath on screen, with soft sounds and haptics — it keeps working even with the screen off.

MEDITATION TIMER
Opening and closing gong, optional interval bells, calm ambient sound. Choose ocean surf, a gentle breeze, a quiet breathing sound — or silence.

YOUR PROGRESS
Your streak and minutes are stored only on your phone. Optionally, sessions are written to Apple Health as mindful minutes.

PRIVACY
Breathwave collects no data. Zero. It does not even connect to the internet.

Breathwave is free and has no ads. If it helps you, you can support development with a voluntary tip inside the app.
```

## Privacy labels (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

Odpověď: **Data Not Collected** — žádná analytika, žádný crash reporting, žádný backend.
HealthKit data se zapisují, nikdy nečtou; zůstávají v zařízení → nejde o „collection".
Tip jar řeší Apple (StoreKit), my k platebním údajům nemáme přístup.
